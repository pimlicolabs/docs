# How to bridge and call contracts

This guide shows you how to bridge USDC and call contracts on the destination chain in the same request, for example to swap it for another token. To only deliver the USDC to an address, see [How to bridge to a recipient](/guides/how-to/fast-bridge/bridge-tokens).

## Prerequisites

- A Pimlico API key with Fast Bridge enabled.
- An account holding USDC on the origin chain.
- An EVM destination chain.

## Destination chain calls run from the multicaller

On the destination chain, your calls are sent by Fast Bridge's `multicaller` contract, not by the user. Every contract you call sees the `multicaller` as `msg.sender`, and the bridged USDC sits there when your calls start.

That means:

- A contract that pulls tokens needs an approval from the `multicaller` first, like the swap router in the example.
- Anything meant for the user has to name them, like the swap's `recipient`.
- A contract that only acts for its caller, such as a withdrawal or a cancellation, won't work.

You can look up the `multicaller` address with [`GET /addresses/{chain}`](/references/fast-bridge/endpoints#get-addresseschain).
