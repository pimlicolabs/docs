# How to accept USDC at checkout without customers paying gas

This guide shows you how to accept USDC at checkout without customers holding ETH: you **sponsor the customer's USDC transfer** with Pimlico's verifying paymaster, tie it to the order through its nonce key, and **verify the payment on your backend** from the user operation receipt. The USDC moves directly from the customer's account to your merchant address; Pimlico doesn't hold funds. If you'd rather not pay the gas, have the customer pay it in USDC instead; see [How to send USDC without ETH for gas](/guides/how-to/payments/send-usdc-without-eth).

## Steps

::::steps

### Create a checkout sponsorship policy

Create a policy in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.pimlico.io/sponsorship-policies) or with the [API](/references/platform/api/sponsorship-policies/create). For checkout, set:

- a **per-user** limit, for example a small daily `maximum_user_operation_count`, so a single account can't burn your balance,
- a **per-user-operation** spending cap that comfortably covers one USDC transfer on your chain,
- a **global** spending limit with a `reset_interval`,
- `chain_ids.allowlist` with the chains you accept payments on, for example `[8453]` for Base.

To sponsor only payments to your own address, add a [policy webhook](/guides/how-to/sponsorship-policies/webhook) that decodes the user operation's call data and answers `{ "sponsor": true }` only for a USDC `transfer` whose recipient is your merchant address. The `@pimlico/webhook` package provides both signature verification and a `parseCallData` helper for this. For a complete handler to adapt, see [How to send USDC remittances without ETH for gas](/guides/how-to/payments/remittances).

### Put your API key behind a proxy

The checkout code runs in the customer's browser, so it must not contain your Pimlico API key. Run a small proxy on your backend that authenticates the shopper's session, forwards JSON-RPC requests to `https://api.pimlico.io/v2/8453/rpc?apikey=...`, and rejects anything you don't expect. In the [API keys page](https://dashboard.pimlico.io/apikeys), restrict the key to your proxy's IP addresses and to the method groups you use. See [How to protect your API keys](/guides/how-to/security/protect-api-keys) for an Express and a Fastify example.

Store the `userOpHash` with the order and make it unique, so the same payment can't be reported for two orders.

## Accept payment from an external wallet

Customers using a browser extension wallet usually can't sign an EIP-7702 authorization for your app. If their wallet supports EIP-5792 `wallet_sendCalls` with the `paymasterService` capability (ERC-7677), you can still sponsor the payment: pass your proxy URL as the paymaster service and your policy ID in its `context`.

Here you receive a transaction hash rather than a user operation hash, and the nonce key isn't under your control. Verify by fetching the transaction receipt from your RPC, parsing USDC `Transfer` logs with `parseEventLogs`, and checking `from` is the customer's address, `to` is your merchant address and the value covers the order. Store the transaction hash with the order so it can't be reused. See [external wallets](/guides/eip7702/external) for a wagmi version.

## Operational notes

- **Pending payments**: if the receipt is slow, check [`pimlico_getUserOperationStatus`](/references/bundler/endpoints/pimlico_getUserOperationStatus). It distinguishes `not_submitted`, `submitted`, `included`, `rejected` and `failed`, and keeps statuses for one hour.
- **Sponsorship validity**: a sponsorship signature is valid for 10 minutes by default. If customers might leave the confirmation screen open longer, prepare the user operation again or extend the window with `validForSeconds` ([guide](/guides/how-to/paymasters/extending-sponsorship-duration)).
- **Refunds and overpayments** are your application's responsibility; send them from your merchant account like any other USDC transfer.
- **Billing**: keep your Pimlico balance or card able to cover sponsored checkouts; sponsored gas is charged there ([verifying paymaster FAQ](/references/paymaster/verifying-paymaster/faqs)).
