# Pimlico Paymasters

Pimlico provides two different types of paymasters to allow you to abstract away the gas fees for your users on EVM chains, and Solana Sponsorships to do the same on Solana.

## Verifying Paymaster

Our Verifying Paymaster sponsors the on-chain gas fees for your users on 100+ chains. It needs no upfront funding: add a card in [the dashboard](https://dashboard.pimlico.io) and pay for usage at the end of each month, or pre-load a balance there.

## ERC-20 Paymaster

Our ERC-20 Paymaster is a permissionless on-chain smart contract that lets your users pay for their own gas fees using their ERC-20 tokens.

## Solana Sponsorships

Solana Sponsorships lets you sponsor the transaction fees of your users on Solana, either in full or with the user paying in USDC or USDT inside the same transaction. See the [Solana sponsorship guides](/guides/how-to/solana) and the [endpoint reference](/references/paymaster/solana/endpoints).
